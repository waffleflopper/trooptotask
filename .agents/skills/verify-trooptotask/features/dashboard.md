# Dashboard composition and freshness

The Organization Dashboard composes authorized Personnel, Calendar, Training, Critical Tasks, Onboarding and Rating facts. A source's permission remains independent; active scoped Personnel supply strength, while the Rating due projection omits archived People's evaluations (the stored rows remain). The shell must remain usable while the workspace/summary load, and old responses must never restore another Organization's facts.

## Sub-features

- `strength-band`: Bottom line, the 14-day forecast with its floor line and flagged duty days, and the by-group strip, all from one per-day status index over the loaded window. Steps are under [Strength band](#strength-band).
- `quick-add`: Status, Training record and Start onboarding beside the title open the Calendar, Training and Onboarding dialogs, and each save shows on the Dashboard without a reload. Steps are under [Quick add](#quick-add).
- `needs-you-actions`: Update status, Record class, Fill, Check off and Award started act on a Needs you row in place, and a saved row leaves the queue. Steps are under [Actions](#actions).
- `combined-overview`: current strength, availability, Person/Group assignments, Training attention/currency, evaluations, Onboarding and nearest ETS/DEROS use independently known source facts.
- `source-admission`: full, Alpha-only, empty-Group and no-lane members have different authorized projections; an ordinary foreign member cannot read A.
- `readiness-data`: Critical Tasks summary is authorized and scoped even though the compact Overview currently renders no Critical Tasks card. Inspect its ordinary page response separately from visible UI.
- `reload-and-consumers`: counts persist after reload and navigation to source pages; hydration cannot corrupt the source workspaces.
- `navigation-recovery`: an exact A Dashboard navigation fails once, remains recoverable, and a delayed A navigation cannot overwrite B.
- `deferred-state`: first-paint shell, workspace/summary pending, rejection and scope replacement remain explicit obligations. Whole-navigation holds do not prove individual server-source delay or failure.

After changing request composition, run the real Organization Home route with Critical Tasks access and inspect its settled summary as well as the visible Overview. Tests that supply a broad fixture context or mock the summary operation cannot establish that the route selected every required provider. The Dashboard must settle without its load-error alert before this path earns verification credit.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in, enter an Organization and choose **Home** in its sidebar. The Organization breadcrumb also returns to this Dashboard. Its source workspaces remain available through Calendar, Personnel, Training, ICTLs and Onboard when the member has the corresponding access. Use **Switch organization** to select another membership; it preserves the current section, so choose Home afterward when switching from Calendar.

## Frame

The page opens with a header and three bands, each under a 2px rule. The eyebrow reads `DASHBOARD · <UNIT> · <N> PERSONNEL` once the data is in and drops the count while loading. The title is the viewer day, such as `THURSDAY 22 OCT`. Quick add appears beside the title only when an active section offers an entry. `Strength` holds Bottom line (400px) and the 14-day forecast with the by-group strip under it, `Action` holds Needs you and On duty (340px), and `Readiness` holds one column per active section that has one. Below 1024px the right-hand column stacks under the left. Needs you shows up to nine rows; from ten it shows seven and a fold row whose `Show all N` expands in place. A row narrower than 672px puts its label and timing on the first line. On duty holds Tonight and tomorrow, one row per Calendar lane in lane order, and Coming and going for the next seven days. The Training & Evaluations and Onboarding cards still render under the bands until later slices replace them. A section whose feature is switched off contributes no row, lane, column or button.

While the workspace or summary is pending, each band shows skeletons shaped like its content and one visually hidden `role="status"` reads `Loading Dashboard`. A failed summary source keeps the header and replaces the bands with `WorkspaceLoadError`. A failed workspace source is caught by the Dashboard layout, which replaces the whole page. Steps live in [Dashboard and Training states](training-form-states.md#driving-it-with-verifyapp).

## Strength band

A Person counts once a day. When entries overlap, the status type first in catalog order wins, then the entry that started first. Assigned is the active scoped roster. A duty day is a weekday that is not a stored holiday or closure in `special_days`; the bootstrap reads those in the same `Promise.all` as the other workspace sources, so a `special_days` hold or `fail-hard` behaves like `availability_entries` in step 8 and a failure replaces the page through the Dashboard layout. A duty day below the Organization's strength floor (Settings › Features, 80% by default) is flagged: amber count and label, and one amber band across each stretch of adjacent flagged columns. Weekends and closures stay dimmed and are never flagged, even below the floor, so a run that crosses a weekend shows two bands while the headline counts it as one run. A flagged today turns the hero number amber. The range and lowest day in `Steady` and `N to M available` come from duty days only, and a flagged duty day always outranks `Steady`.

- The forecast flags today and every other duty day Baker is out; the headline reads `Below 80% today, back above on` the first duty day after Baker returns, by weekday alone when it is within a week. The legend lists Verification Training and `80% floor`.
- The by-group strip reads `BRAVO 0/1` (amber) then `ALPHA 2/2`. `Open calendar` opens A's Calendar on the month of the Dashboard's today, even if Calendar was left on another month.
- `dashboardScoped` reads `2`, `of 2`, `AVAILABLE · 100%`, `Everyone is available today.` and `Steady: 2 available every day`, and the strip shows only `ALPHA 2/2`.

Scale comes from the workloads. On 2026-10-21 `perfWide` read `15 of 20` (`small`, one block per Person, names and return dates, `ALPHA 7/10` amber and `BRAVO 8/10`), `86 of 100` (`typical`, one proportional bar, one row per status with a count, the two largest source Groups and `N more` Groups, an `All 14 names` toggle, and `By group · all 5 · lowest first`) and `295 of 400` (`large`, `By group · lowest 5 of 16` and `All 16 groups`). Up to 40 People the strip draws a block per Person; above 40 it draws counts. Up to six Groups all show; above six the lowest five show, at any roster size. ## Actions

A Needs you row offers one action when the viewer may take it; otherwise only its link remains. Overdue offers `Update status` (`ratingScheme.updateEntries`), Expired `Record class` (`training.recordCompletions`), Unfilled `Fill N days` (`calendar.manageAssignments`, N at most 7), Stalled `Check off` (`onboarding.manageWorkflows`) and Leaving `Award started` (`personnel.manageRecords` on that Person). `Check off` is a link to Onboarding with that packet open, because completing a step needs the Onboarding workspace. While an action opens or saves, its button reads `aria-disabled="true"` and ignores a second press. A failed save keeps the row and shows the message in it, as a `role="alert"` line or inside the open dialog or panel, with no toast. A saved row leaves the queue and focus moves to the action, or else the link, of the row now in its place. Closing a dialog or panel returns focus to its button. None of these reads anything at page load.
