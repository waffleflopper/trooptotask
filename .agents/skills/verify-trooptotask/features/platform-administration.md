# Platform access, accounts and Organizations

Platform support investigates accounts, Organization membership and reviewed access; super-admins additionally suspend targets and administer Organization roles without granting ordinary users platform authority.

## Sub-features

- `admission`: active super-admin/support access; ordinary, inactive and billing-role denial to Users, Organizations and Access Requests.
- `directory`: search, paging, account and Organization detail, current membership, setup/activation and suspension state.
- `reviewed-access`: pending approval/rejection, rescind of approved incomplete access, invitation origin and approval basis, blocked linked invitations, setup delivery/replacement and completed-setup protection.
- `administration`: super-admin suspension/recovery, member promotion, confirmed ownership transfer and denial without mutation.
- `recovery`: retained drafts, failed requests, explicit retry, persistence and correct target after navigation.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in as an active platform administrator and open Admin. Choose Users, Organizations or Access Requests in the sidebar. Search the directory and follow a record link to inspect its detail. Super-admins use Suspend/Unsuspend (in `More User controls` and `More Organization controls`) and Organization member actions; support can investigate and manage reviewed access but cannot perform suspension, promotion or ownership transfer.

## Gotchas

- D21 owns access/accounts/Organizations/search/suspension and their source operations. D22 owns announcements, Feedback, Audit-support composition and Dashboard; reuse D19's safe recent-activity evidence without claiming D22 completion.
- The owned app now delivers setup email to a private synthetic mail sink (`mail state`, `mail fail`; see [Organization invitations](organization-invitations.md)), so an unconfigured-delivery outcome is no longer reachable and a delivery failure needs `mail fail`. Approval, link issuance, outbox delivery, setup completion and invitation acceptance are different effects; capture the actual state.
- Support can review access and send account support emails; only super-admin can suspend or change Organization roles. An inactive platform row grants nothing. The retained billing role admits Dashboard only.
- User suspension uses `user_suspensions`, not provider bans. Organization suspension uses `organizations.suspended_at`; preserve the actual restricted shell and denial of writes against the retained suspension contract. Both suspensions also deny page data loads and members' direct table and Storage access, while the Organization row and own membership stay readable for the shell. `organization_suspension_rls.sql` owns the direct SQL proof.
- Provider/SQL failures and concurrent transfer guarantees need separate lower-level proof. The owned app's fresh chain is not a substitute for an affected isolated SQL contract run.
