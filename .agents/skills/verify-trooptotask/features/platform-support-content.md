# Platform announcements, Feedback and Audit support

Platform administrators publish global messages, review member Feedback and investigate permitted activity. Support and retained billing accounts receive only their admitted pages and reduced activity projections.

## Sub-features

- `admission`: super-admin admits Dashboard, Announcements, Feedback and full Audit; support admits Dashboard and Feedback, billing admits Dashboard only, and inactive or ordinary accounts admit none.
- `announcement-lifecycle`: create Info, Warning and Maintenance messages, optional expiration, cancellation, activate/deactivate, confirmed deletion, and persisted member banners and account-wide dismissals.
- `feedback-review`: retain submitter/context/message while changing New, Reviewed and Resolved status, save or clear internal Notes, reload and recover from failure.
- `audit-investigation`: server pages of 50, action/severity/Organization filters, details, masking and reduced Dashboard activity.
- `dashboard-and-navigation`: current platform counts, pending Feedback, recent activity and retained search/navigation without widening disclosure.
- `failure-and-lifetime`: failed writes preserve the unsaved choice, release pending controls and permit an explicit retry; late results cannot affect another page or record.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in to the internal admin workspace at `/admin`. Use Dashboard, Feedback, Audit Log and Announcements in its sidebar. Select Create Announcement, complete its fields and Save. Deactivate or Activate changes its member visibility. Delete requires the browser confirmation. Expand a Feedback card to change Status or Admin Notes, then choose Save Notes. Audit Log provides Action and Severity controls, Previous/Next pages and View details. The global search opens the already owned User and Organization support pages.

## Gotchas

- Global announcements and account dismissals differ from Organization-scoped private communications. An Organization role grants no platform privilege.
- Full Audit belongs to super-admin. Support's recent activity does not contain details, IP or user-agent, even when a stored event contains those fields.
- A seeded masked event proves display/paging, not producer sanitization. Announcement audit events and real Feedback submission establish their own producer effects.
- Announcements currently have create, toggle and delete controls, with no editing form. Feedback has no delete control. Do not invent a journey for either.
- Synthetic 503s exercise browser response handling. Logged-empty provider list errors require their own proof and remain a cutover blocker.
- D19 owns Audit storage/projection policy, D20 owns member communications, and D21 owns accounts/Organizations/search. D22 owns remaining admin content/Dashboard composition and removal of their replaced central paths. D24/D25/D28 retain export/job/provider infrastructure.
