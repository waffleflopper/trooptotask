# Onboarding templates and configuration

An Organization Onboarding configurator defines reusable checklists with checkbox, paperwork and Training steps. Definitions are shared within the Organization; configuring them requires Organization scope and works without Personnel or Training capability.

## Sub-features

- `templates`: create, rename in place, clear nullable descriptions, cancel, duplicate and delete a template while preserving retained packet snapshots and unrelated definitions; search by template or step name; active packet counts.
- `steps`: create/edit/delete checkbox, paperwork and Training steps in one in-place editor with a discard prompt; preserve ordered paperwork stages and same-Organization Training references; reorder by grip (pointer drag or ArrowUp/ArrowDown with a spoken announcement) without changing step identity.
- `sync`: `Sync active packets` re-syncs every in-progress packet of the template and reports one honest result.
- `lookups`: Onboarding receives scoped Personnel references and Organization Training labels through their owned lookup seams, independently of those lanes' grants.
- `access-and-recovery`: Organization-scoped configure permits definition writes; view/operate and Group-scoped configure cannot mutate the catalog. Foreign references and stale Organization responses cannot cross Organizations. Failed requests need visible feedback, retained drafts and real retry.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose `Onboard` in Organization navigation, then the `Templates` action in the Active page toolbar (configurers only). The page opens with the `Templates` title (an `h1`) and the line `The steps each new arrival works through. Edits apply to new packets right away; active packets update when you sync them.`; the toolbar sits under it. It holds the search `Search templates and steps`, the `Active onboarding` link back to the packet workspace and the primary `New template`. Each list row reads `{k} steps · {m} active packets` (or `no active packets`); `m` counts only in-progress packets, so completed history is not counted. The list is headed `Templates` with the row count. Select a row to open the editor: the eyebrow `Template · {k} steps`, the name (an `h2`, focusable by script) with a pencil `Rename {name}`, an editable description, `Duplicate`, a destructive `Delete`, a `Sync active packets` notice when `m` is above 0, then the `Steps` heading with the caption `in the order Soldiers see them · drag to reorder` and the step list. Each step row has a grip `Reorder {step}`, its number, name, a detail line, its type, and `Edit {step}` and `Delete {step}` icon buttons; `Add step` closes the list. Below 1024 the list and the editor swap and a `Templates` back button returns. The direct `onboarding/templates` route requires Organization-scoped configuration authority; every other actor gets a 403 page. View/operate members read shared definitions through the ordinary Onboarding workspace and its template labels instead.

## Driving it with verify:app

Preconditions: `doctor` passes for this checkout and worker. `$V fixture` provides `onboardingTemplate`, three distinguishable A steps (`onboardingCheckbox`, `onboardingPaperwork`, `onboardingTraining`), B's template/step, and `onboardingPacket` with three completed progress snapshots for Able. The completed packet avoids changing active Calendar markers. Seeded A steps are Orientation checkbox, Paperwork with Submitted → Reviewed → Filed stages, and Training Step linked to Required Training, in order 0/1/2. B's private Training step links only to B's private Training type. Dedicated `onboardingConfigure`, `onboardingOperate`, `onboardingView`, `onboardingScoped`, `onboardingEmpty`, `onboardingForeign` and `onboardingSwitch` have all other feature lanes absent; Switch configures A and views B. Scoped grants Alpha; Empty grants no Groups. Use `$V actor ROLE onboarding` (or `onboarding/templates`, `onboarding/history`), record its page/Organization, and inspect actual loaded data. Arrival is not acceptance.

1. **Entry and independent lookups:** As `onboardingConfigure`, enter through Onboard → Templates. Capture seeded names/descriptions, all three step meanings and order. In a temporary Training step, open the Training Type selector and verify A's Required/Targeted/Optional labels, no B label, despite Training and Personnel being absent. Cancel without a write. In Onboarding, open Start Onboarding and inspect Personnel choices: Able, Baker and Unassigned are candidates because completed history does not prevent a new packet; Able's completed history remains readable. The seeded template/steps already satisfy the Start Onboarding prerequisite. Add Person must be absent for these Onboarding-only actors; receiving Personnel lookup labels does not grant Personnel creation. Cancel without starting a packet. As Scoped, shared template labels remain A's but Personnel choices/history are Alpha-only; Empty has no Personnel. Record observed choices and bound lookup results separately from direct raw-table reads. The [Personnel lifecycle recipe](personnel-records-lifecycle.md) owns authorized Onboarding → Add Person rank selection, retired/foreign option exclusion and the separate valid authenticated creation-denial probe; those expected instructions do not establish a live pass.
2. **Template lifecycle:** `New template` opens a form with `Name` focused, `Description`, `Cancel` and `Create template`. Create a unique template with description, capture save/reload and `$V rows onboarding_templates orgA name MARKER`; the new row is selected and focused. Choose the pencil `Rename {name}`: name and description become one form (`Save template`); Enter or the button saves and Escape cancels, and focus returns to the pencil. Edit both on the same ID; reload/read back. Clear the description and save; the row reads `None` (null). Change a separate draft then Cancel; compare exact rows to the previous save. While this form or a step editor is open, `New template`, the pencil, `Duplicate` and `Delete` are disabled. Delete this unlinked template through confirmation at the end and read back absence. Do not infer a save from a closed form. Cancelling `New template` returns to the first template, not the one open before.
3. **All step meanings:** On the new template, `Add step` creates checkbox, paperwork and Training steps with descriptions in the in-place editor (`Editing step`, or `New step` for a new one; one editor opens at a time). The `Type` radios `Checkbox`, `Paperwork` and `Training` switch the fields; the chosen one is filled (primary colors) in light and dark, the others are not. Paperwork gets three unique stages (type a `Stage name`, Enter or `Add stage`), reordered with the stage grips `Reorder {stage}` by keyboard and by pointer drag; remove one with `Remove {stage}` and Cancel a separate draft. Training requires a selected A type (`Training Type`). Edit a saved step with `Edit {step}` and change types (Training → checkbox → paperwork); irrelevant Training IDs/stages clear, the same step ID survives, and the saved type/description/stages reload correctly. Compare visible order and `$V rows onboarding_template_steps orgA template_id ID` sort orders/IDs. Delete only a temporary step with `Delete {step}`: the dialog reads `Delete {step}? Active packets get this change when you sync them.`; verify cancellation leaves it, then confirm deletion and read back absence (remaining sort orders can keep a gap).
4. **Duplicate:** Choose the editor's `Duplicate` on the seeded A template, then on it again (each copy opens selected, so reselect the source). Expect distinct copy names (`… Copy`) and new template/step IDs; descriptions, types, ordered stages, Training IDs and source order are retained. Compare copies and unchanged source via readback, reload and visibly inspect a copy. Delete copies visibly/read back absence. Each Duplicate press sends a new request id once its answer arrives. Step 15 covers a lost answer.
5. **Failure and retry:** Arm `$V network arm-fail $PAGE onboarding-templates` before opening the create/edit form. Onboarding controls reload the current page to install before the HTTP adapter captures fetch, so fill the draft after arming. Save; capture matched503, visible error, retained draft, no success and exact unchanged provider rows. Restore, retry visibly, reload and compare one saved row or the same edited ID. Each `arm` reloads the page and drops the open template, so select the template again after arming. Repeat one step edit with `onboarding-template-steps` (an `Alert` shows the message and the editor keeps its draft), reorder with `onboarding-template-steps/reorder` (the grips return to the saved order and an `Alert` shows), duplicate with `onboarding-templates/ID/duplicate`, and delete confirmation on a temporary template. The request family matches A's exact endpoint only; restore before another control. Retained data/retry may pass while missing visible error feedback fails. Keep that failed observation and its owning repair; do not redefine success.
6. **Access and references:** View/Operate must lack Templates and the direct config route; a no-Onboarding `viewer` must be denied the parent. `onboardingView`, `onboardingScoped` and `onboardingEmpty` each load the direct route as an `Error 403` page (capture all three). A Group-scoped configurator receives403 on valid template create/edit/delete, step create/edit/delete, reorder and duplicate requests. Reorder must include exactly the existing A step IDs; invalid400/409 is not authorization proof. For each denied action compare exact templates/steps/packets/progress before/after. Organization configure must reject B route writes and A writes with B template/Training references. Switch's B view membership must not grant B configure or permit changing A row ownership/identity. Owner/admin retain configuration authority. Ordinary signed-in API probes and raw provider readbacks are separate from UI proof.
7. **Switch and retention:** As Switch, use the visible Organization menu to change A → B → A. B's empty packet workspace shows no A data and no configuration controls; A returns with A values. Inspect B's private template/Training label separately as Foreign in B configuration. B lookup responses may provide separate API proof, but the empty packet screen does not render those catalog/Personnel labels. Hold a real A config mutation response only after persistence using `arm-hold`, switch visibly to B, release, and inspect/reload both Organizations for stale rows/drafts/controls; retain an observed failure. After all seeded-dependent journeys, edit the seeded template/step and compare linked packet/progress IDs and snapshot fields; configuration edits do not automatically resync completed history. Restore original editable fields through UI. Finally delete the seeded checkbox step and template through visible confirmations. The linked packet and all progress snapshot IDs/content remain; only deleted definition links become null. Open History/reload to verify retained names/status. Restart this owned worker before any journey that needs the original seed again.
8. **Type-management add, edit, delete and confirm:** On Templates as `onboardingConfigure`, add a uniquely named template through the shared type-management list/detail workspace, select it in the list, rename it with the pencil and save, then reload and read back the same ID. Choose the header `Delete`: the dialog reads `Delete {name}? Its steps are deleted.`, and with in-progress packets it adds `{m} active packet(s) keep their steps but lose their link to this template.` (singular for one). Cancel first and require the template, selection and focus on the opening control to remain; confirm a second attempt and require the list entry and `$V rows onboarding_templates orgA name MARKER` to be absent. With no template left the list reads `No templates yet.` with `New template`, and the editor pane reads `Select a template`.
9. **Reorder by grip:** With three steps, focus `Reorder {step}` and press ArrowUp or ArrowDown: the step moves, focus stays on its grip and a polite status reads `{step} moved to position {n} of {k}`. Reload and compare sort orders. A real pointer `drag` from one grip onto another step also reorders (the drag tool dispatches real pointer events). With a paperwork editor open, the stage grips reorder stages the same way and only the saved step persists them; the step list's grips and the stage list's grips are separate lists. With `onboarding-template-steps/reorder` armed to fail, the order snaps back and an `Alert` shows.
10. **Discard prompt:** Open `Edit {step A}`, change its name without saving, then choose `Edit {step B}`. `Discard unsaved changes?` (an `alertdialog`) offers `Cancel`, which keeps the open edit and its draft, and `Discard`, which drops it and opens step B. An editor `Cancel` button discards without a prompt. An unchanged editor swaps without a prompt.
11. **Counts and sync:** Run `$V seed onboarding-consumers`, reload Templates: the seeded template reads `3 steps · 2 active packets` (the completed packet is not counted) and a notice `2 active packets use this template` shows `Sync active packets`. To prove a partial result, `$V network arm-fail $PAGE onboarding-resync 2` (the second resync fails), select the template again and choose `Sync active packets`: the status reads `Synced 1 of 2. 1 could not be synced. Try again.` After `network restore`, choose it again: `Synced 2 packets`. With no in-progress packet the notice is absent. The `outside your access` wording is unreachable through the route (only Organization-scoped configurators load it); the component test covers it. Run `clean-seeds` and reload: the row reads `no active packets`.
12. **Search:** Type a template name or a step name into `Search templates and steps`; the list keeps rows whose template or any step matches, and a miss reads `No template or step is named like "…".` Clear with real keystrokes (Backspace); a native fill of an empty string leaves the filter applied.
13. **Saves that overlap:** With two templates, each with two or more steps, arm `$V network arm-hold $PAGE onboarding-template-steps/reorder`, then select the second template again (arming reloads). Reorder one step with its grip, read the held request in `network state`, `network release`, and reload: the new order persists. Repeat, but while the first template's reorder is held select the other template and reorder one of its steps too; release and reload both: each template keeps its own order (only one reorder is sent per template and the second is not dropped). With the reorder held, `Edit {step}`, rename it and `Save step`, then release: the renamed step keeps its new name and the reordered position. Arm `onboarding-template-steps` with `arm-hold` instead, edit a step name and `Save step`, and while it is held choose `Add step`: the unsaved-changes prompt appears; `Discard` opens the new step form, type a name, then `network release`: the old step shows its saved name and the new form keeps the text and the focus. Restore every control and delete the temporary rows.
14. **Narrow focus:** At 390 (below 1024) the list and editor take turns. Select a row with Enter: focus moves to the template name (`h2`, `tabindex="-1"`), and `Templates` returns focus to that row. Create a template (`New template`, name, `Create template`): focus lands on the new template's name. Delete a template through the confirmation: the list returns with focus on the selected row. Read `document.activeElement` with `evaluate_script` after each; a focus that falls back to `body` fails.

15. **Provider command faults (duplicate, reorder, delete):** Client 503 controls fail before the request reaches the provider, so they cannot show a command that commits and then loses its answer. Duplicate, reorder and template delete are each one database command (`rpc/duplicate_onboarding_template`, `rpc/reorder_onboarding_template_steps`, `rpc/delete_onboarding_template`). `onboarding-template-duplicate|reorder|delete` fail the command before it reaches the provider. The `-lost-ack` form of each lets it commit, then replaces the answer with a 503; `$V fault state` records `ordinal`, `matches`, `phase`, the method and path and, for a lost answer, `providerStatus`. Arm one, run exactly one native action on a temporary template and restore before the next. Before each case save the snapshot and `$V rows` for the template, its steps (ID, `sort_order`) and, for delete, a started packet and its progress rows. After the action capture the HTTP status, the visible message, `fault state`, exact rows and a reload. Intended outcomes: a stopped duplicate, reorder or delete leaves every row exactly as it was, and the reorder grip order returns to the saved one. A lost duplicate answer shows `Copy not confirmed` naming the source with `Retry`; reload the page, confirm the notice is still there, restore and choose `Retry`. Read back exactly one new copy, and expect the notice to be gone and the copy selected. Repeat the lost answer, then delete the source before retrying. The retry still returns the saved copy. A lost delete answer leaves the template on screen with an error; after restore, deleting it again succeeds and reads back absent. The app writes no audit row for a lost-answer delete or duplicate, because the retry finds nothing new to record; capture `audit_logs` to confirm this known gap. Compare unrelated templates, steps, packets and progress before and after; on delete the only intended change is the packet's `template_id` and its progress rows' `template_step_id` becoming null. Arm `onboarding-steps-insert|update|delete` during a duplicate, reorder and template delete to show those commands make no table writes from the app: each control stays at `matches: 0` and the action succeeds. Rollback, lock waits and authority after waits are proven by `supabase/tests/onboarding_template_commands.sql` and `onboarding_template_command_concurrency.sql`, not by these faults. Run `node --test scripts/verification/outbound-controls.test.mjs` for the fault boundaries.

For each saved change keep before/action/after snapshots and a screenshot, second visible read after reload/reopen, request status where relevant and exact provider readback. The existing `$V rows` helper allows `onboarding_templates`, `onboarding_template_steps`, `personnel_onboardings`, and `onboarding_step_progress` in `orgA|orgB`; use `template_id`, `onboarding_id`, `template_step_id`, `id` or `name` filters. Compare complete saved Template keys `id/orgId/name/description/createdAt` and Step keys `id/templateId/name/description/stepType/trainingTypeId/stages/sortOrder/organization_id/created_at`. Template `createdAt` may be null; preserve Step metadata spellings. Keep full raw provider rows separate from these response projections. Store sanitized proof under the skill's durable evidence root and explicitly distinguish live-verified, failed, source-mapped and unreachable paths.

## Gotchas

- Approved Onboarding configure is Organization-scoped. Configuration navigation, direct page admission and template/step mutations must all require Organization scope. The unchanged baseline exposed unscoped controls/reorder admission; compare the repaired UI/request/stored-row outcomes against that recorded failure.
- The workspace retains mutation errors internally; only a rendered message establishes user feedback. A synthetic503 is client delivery proof, not partial-provider transaction proof.
- The theme sets `data-theme` on `html`; the header `Toggle theme` button flips it and stores `localStorage.theme`. Read the attribute, not a `dark` class.
- A search filter hides rows only; the open template stays in the editor even when it no longer matches.
- Select portal options may be absent from accessibility snapshots. Inspect rendered options read-only and use focused arrows/Enter; verify the selected label and saved reference.
- Use focused keyboard deletion for bound text clears. A native fill of an empty string alone may clear the DOM without updating the Svelte draft.
- Seeded packet progress is a retained snapshot. Do not require automatic resync or expand this recipe into D15's full packet lifecycle or D12's Personnel creation.
- Restore armed network/feedback controls and UI edits; delete UI-created rows visibly/read back absence. `clean-seeds` removes only helper-created rows. Finish with owned cleanup and verify artifacts survive.
- Existing installed-SDK persistence/CRUD/query/workspace/page/duplicate tests, `supabase/tests/onboarding_template_rls.sql` and the two `onboarding_template_command*.sql` files establish distinct lower-level boundaries; neither source review nor direct API proof replaces visible interaction.
