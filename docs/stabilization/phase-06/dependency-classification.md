Each of the 14 relations has its effective ACL. There are no column-specific ACLs on these tables. ## FK-derived triggers on retained relations

The raw replay contains 70 internal FK triggers attached to otherwise retained relations: 28 on `organizations`, 12 on `personnel`, four on `assignment_types`, two on `daily_assignments`, and 24 on `auth.users`. The 24 Auth triggers belong to 12 Duty-owned FKs and appear in the catalog's `dependencies` section; the public-only `triggers` section omits their definitions. Each entry in the manifest's `fk_derived_triggers` section names the exact trigger and its owning FK constraint from the internal dependency edge. The 68 on `organizations`, `personnel`, `assignment_types` and `auth.users` retire with Duty-owned FKs under D02 06-03. The two on `daily_assignments` retire with its lineage FK under D02 06-02. Recount every internal trigger dependency for all 54 crossing FKs: 216 edges equal 146 Duty-attached triggers plus these 70 reverse triggers, with no missing or multiply classified reverse identity. PostgreSQL removes them with the constraints; D02 must compare the post-cutover catalog by FK ownership, not expect OID-derived trigger names to survive. The retained parent relations and their unrelated triggers remain.

The four retained relations `public.daily_assignments`, `public.assignment_types`, `public.status_types`, and `public.sign_in_rosters` keep their current non-Duty columns, identity/uniqueness constraints, ACLs and RLS. The manifest assigns 119 exact retained relation/attached-object/function identities, excluding the eight explicit shared-cutover entries and 70 FK-derived triggers. The Calendar policies use the exact retained helpers `can_edit_daily_assignment_record(uuid,text)`, `can_edit_daily_assignment_record(uuid,uuid,text)`, `can_view_daily_assignment_record(uuid,text)`, `can_view_daily_assignment_record(uuid,uuid,text)`, `can_manage_calendar_configuration(uuid)` and `can_view_calendar(uuid)`. `public.sign_in_rosters` is an independent Training workflow, despite its name. Keep its four policies and the exact functions `can_access_sign_in_roster_scope(uuid,uuid[],text)`, `can_access_sign_in_roster(uuid,uuid[],uuid[],jsonb,text)` and `sign_in_roster_scope_matches_personnel(uuid,uuid[],uuid[],jsonb)`. These six retained helpers plus the shared-cutover rank RPC `delete_unused_organization_rank_catalog_entry(uuid,uuid,text)` account for every non-Duty function-body text hit in the replay. The four public enums are unrelated to Duty. The catalog has zero cron jobs and no Duty event trigger.
