# Manage Personnel records

A member creates and edits a Soldier's record, imports new People or backfills existing dates, and archives or restores a record while keeping its identity, permissions and retained history intact.

## Sub-features

- `record-create-edit`: create through Personnel and Onboarding, edit through the roster modal and detail page, cancel or recover a failed save, and retain the Person ID through name, rank and Group changes.
- `record-import`: upload a roster file, map columns, correct invalid cells, select valid rows, preserve row errors and recover a failed request without claiming an unsaved record.
- `record-backfill`: match active People by rank/name, disambiguate duplicate names by Group, update only supplied ETS/DEROS dates and skip an unchanged replay.
- `record-archive-restore`: single and bulk archive remove People from active views; restore retains their IDs and restores only artifacts archived by that parent event.
- `record-approval`: an operate-only member requests archive approval without archiving the Person; an authorized reviewer applies the archive once.
- `record-access`: view, operate, configure, assigned Group, empty Group scope and foreign Organization requests preserve their distinct permissions and saved data.
- `record-consumers`: Training, Critical Tasks, Calendar, Onboarding and record exports retain the Person identity and their independent access lanes across record changes.
- `record-detail-readiness`: one scoped header lookup supplies the same Person ID to all artifact tabs; five independent readiness lanes retain their own permissions and facts.
- `record-soldier-data-sheet`: save and reload the existing More info form, recover a failed save, and preserve unrelated acknowledgement fields.
- `record-loss-acknowledgement`: distinguish detail ETS readiness from global ETS/DEROS losses, and set or clear the existing acknowledgement through its authenticated endpoint without inventing a Web toggle.
- `record-retention`: scheduled removal of expired archived parents, complete cascade-file cleanup, retained history, scoped counts and truthful failures.
- `record-switching`: Organization changes and late save responses cannot mix A's Personnel or write permissions into B.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose `Personnel` in the Organization navigation. Use `Add Person`, a named row's `Edit` or `Details`, or `Import`. On the import page choose `Add new` or `Backfill ETS/DEROS`, upload a file and review the mapped rows before submitting. Archive from the named roster row, its Edit dialog, its detail page, or the roster's `Archive` selection mode. An Organization owner/admin restores records through Settings → `Archives`; archive requests appear under Settings → `Approvals`. The reverse creation path is Onboard → `Start Onboarding` → `Add Person`.
