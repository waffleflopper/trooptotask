# Manual assignments

A Calendar operator plans Person and Group coverage for a day or month. Saved assignments keep their Organization, date, type and assignee across Calendar, Planning and report reads; a clear removes only the selected slot.

## Sub-features

- `saved-slots`: the September 15 Person, Group and archived assignments retain their fixture IDs and assignees; Organization B's slot stays private.
- `manual-slot`: set an empty Person and Group slot, reload, change or clear each, and preserve unrelated saved slots.
- `monthly-planning`: navigate months, apply a small Quick Fill batch and clear that type with confirmation.
- `planner-range-retry`: a failed uncovered month load settles with Retry; a successful retry restores editable local assignments.
- `assignment-authorization`: view-only, no-Calendar, Group-scoped and foreign-Organization actors cannot write outside their authority.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in with Calendar operation access and choose `Calendar` → `Planning` → `Assignment Planner`. The monthly table has one named Person or Group control per active assignment type and date. Click a Calendar date header to see the `Daily Assignments` modal for that date. `Reports` shows assignment coverage and other Calendar projections. A Calendar reader may see saved assignments but cannot manage them.
