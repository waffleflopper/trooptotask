Security · access

Built for CUI. Scoped by design.

  • Scoped access, enforced by the server. Organization-wide or by Group, set per feature: none, view, operate or configure.
  • Viewer, Operator and Editor presets, with per-feature overrides. Archive and delete requests need approval.
  • Separate Organizations. One account can switch between units, and their data never mixes.
  • Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Files open through 5-minute private links.
  • MFA, 24-hour sessions, and a verified session for sensitive owner actions. Audit log kept for a year.
  • Aligned with NIST SP 800-171 and hosted on SOC 2 Type II providers. Not FedRAMP-authorized.
