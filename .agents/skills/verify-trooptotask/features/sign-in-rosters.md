# Sign-in rosters

A Training member creates a printable attendance roster from the current Personnel candidates, keeps its saved snapshot, and manages a signed PDF scan without changing Training records.

## Sub-features

- `roster-entry`: open Sign-In Rosters from the Training page and see saved rosters permitted by Training access and Group scope.
- `roster-create`: filter candidates by rank or Group, choose date, sort and layout, generate a PDF, and save the selected Personnel facts.
- `roster-snapshot`: reprint from the saved facts after a selected Person changes; later Personnel edits must not rewrite the saved roster.
- `roster-scan`: upload, download, replace and remove a small synthetic signed PDF in the roster's private file scope.
- `roster-scope`: an Alpha-scoped member sees Alpha-only rosters, while mixed-Group and unassigned snapshots stay outside that member's scope.
- `roster-denied`: Training read access cannot create, upload, remove or delete; no Training access or a foreign Organization cannot read or mutate the roster or scan.
- `roster-failure`: a failed list or scan request shows an error and recovers on retry without deleting the saved roster or file.
- `roster-removal-confirmation`: unlink/delete retains metadata when an existing scan is hidden by an empty ordinary deletion reply; an admitted exact-key check permits retry when the old scan is genuinely absent.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in to Organization A, choose `Training` in the Organization navigation, then `Sign-In Rosters` in the page actions or overflow menu. Choose `New Roster`, enter a title, adjust rank/Group, date, sort and layout choices, then choose `Generate & Print`. Open the saved row to `Re-print`, attach a `Signed Scan`, download its named file, remove the scan, or delete the roster after confirmation.

## Gotchas

- For provider-sized roster history, `$V seed roster-pages` journals and inserts 1,001 Alpha-only A snapshots with fixed microsecond timestamps, null dates, historical names and extra nested marker fields. This is privileged synthetic setup, not native roster creation. Read `$V rows sign_in_rosters orgA` before native JSON/Excel downloads and compare every saved cohort row. Finish all comparisons that need temporary seeds across this worker before `$V clean-seeds`, which removes every journaled seed row, including other cohorts in the same worker. Settings → Ranks → details for `VFY-ACTIVE-A` must count each seeded roster once as a saved filter and once as a historical snapshot, preserving its rank deletion blocker. Existing Personnel/Training references remain separate. Capture the counts before and after cleanup and reload. A successful small export does not prove traversal beyond 1,000 rows.

- For a missing previous scan, use a fresh owned worker and `$V destruction overlap-scan` to remove all Organization A files in both retained buckets through ordinary owner Storage permissions; a fresh fixture has only the Alpha scan, whose saved path remains. Capture the Alpha row and empty `$V roster-files` result. `$V roster-replace "$PAGE" orgA rosterAlpha EVIDENCE_PDF` submits an ordinary multipart replacement from a normally signed-in roster-manager page. Expect success, the new saved path and exact new bytes; reopen the roster and reload to confirm persistence. This is direct authenticated HTTP/provider proof, separate from native upload/removal interaction. Repeat with the viewer to require denial and unchanged rows/files. The old file's absence must be confirmed by the provider; an unacknowledged deletion of an existing file or an unavailable absence check must still fail, with exact cleanup of the new upload or an unresolved upload registration. Native drag-and-drop replacement is not established by this helper.

- The Training entry has a page action and an overflow-menu action; use whichever is visible at the tested viewport. The modal is loaded on demand, so wait for its heading after opening it.
- `Generate & Print` downloads before POSTing the roster. Verify both the PDF and stored row; a generated PDF can exist even when saving fails.
- The server rebuilds snapshot names, ranks and Group labels from selected Personnel IDs. A client-supplied snapshot is not authoritative. Creation is limited to 500 unique Personnel IDs, and out-of-scope selection is rejected before insert.
- A roster containing an unassigned Person is Organization-only. Group-scoped readers need every saved Group within their grant; later Person Group changes do not retroactively alter a stored roster's scope.
- The scan endpoint uses Training read for a signed URL and Training operate for upload/removal, plus roster scope and private Storage checks. A returned signed URL or visible button alone does not prove downloaded bytes or denied direct file access.
- Keep Alpha/Bravo/Unassigned seed rows and private files disposable. Do not edit the maintenance or production database. Restore seeded Person changes and remove UI-created rosters before worker cleanup.
