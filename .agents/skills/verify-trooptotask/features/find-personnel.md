# Find personnel

A member finds a Soldier in the organization roster, switches between group and alphabetical views, and opens the person's record.

## Sub-features

- `roster-search`: match a person by name and return to the full roster by clearing the query.
- `roster-views`: switch between `Group` and `A-Z` presentations.
- `roster-open`: open a matching person's details.
- `roster-readiness`: a Personnel manager with Calendar view access sees the current saved availability in the person's readiness summary.
- `roster-scope`: a group-scoped member sees only their assigned people and Groups.
- `roster-organization`: an Organization-scoped reader sees Alpha, Bravo, and unassigned people, but no foreign Person.
- `roster-group-headers`: in `Group` view each group header has a pin toggle and a collapse toggle, operable by pointer and keyboard; a pin persists for the member and a collapse lasts for the page visit.
- `roster-states`: the roster's empty, no-match, restricted, disabled and narrow-screen states and its absence of URL state.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in, choose `Personnel` in the organization navigation, and use `Search by name, rank, MOS, role, or group`. The `Group` and `A-Z` buttons are above the roster. Choose a person's row or its `Details` action to open the record.

## Driving it with verify:app

Preconditions: doctor passes; `$V fixture` supplies the known Person IDs and current dates; the manager, viewer and scoped actors retain their baseline grants.

1. Run `npm run --silent verify:app -- login manager`; keep the returned page ID as `PAGE`. `take_snapshot` shows the searchbox, `Group` and `A-Z` buttons, and seeded `SPC Able, Synthetic`, `SPC Baker, Synthetic`, and `SPC Unassigned, Synthetic` rows.
2. From a fresh snapshot, use `tool fill` on the searchbox UID with `Able`. Snapshot again: `Open SPC Able, Synthetic` remains and Baker is absent. Focus the searchbox, press `Control+A` then `Backspace` with `tool press_key`, and confirm all seeded rows return. A native `fill` with an empty string can clear the displayed input without dispatching the search update.
3. Click the `A-Z` button UID and snapshot the alphabetical presentation. Click `Group` and confirm the `Alpha`, `Bravo`, and `Unassigned` headings return.
4. Choose the `Open SPC Able, Synthetic` row or its `Details` button using the current snapshot. Confirm the browser moved to that person's detail route and shows the same name. Return through the `Personnel` navigation link.
   For the Calendar-backed readiness check, run `$V calendar-grant manager view` on this owned worker, reload Personnel, and open Baker's record. Its `Readiness summary` must show `Verification Training` through the fixture's `dates.uiAfter`; capture the visible summary and the saved Baker availability row ID from `$V fixture`/`$V rows`. Run `$V calendar-grant manager restore` immediately afterward, including after a failed attempt.
5. Sign in as `viewer` in its own page. The Organization roster must show Able, Baker, and Unassigned, while Foreign is absent. Capture the visible rows and an authenticated read-only request to the current `/personnel/__data.json`; compare the returned text against `personA`, `personB`, `personUnassigned`, and `personForeign` IDs from `$V fixture`. The first three must be present and the foreign ID absent.
6. Sign in as `scoped` in another page. Its Alpha roster must show Able and exclude Baker, Unassigned, and Foreign. In `Group` view only the Alpha header appears. Repeat the page-data ID comparison: only `personA` may appear, and neither the Bravo Group ID nor its name is in the Group catalog or pinned names. Save the visible and page-data observations together. A blank or failed data response is not a passing scope check.
7. **Roster table sort, filter and row action.** As `manager` in `A-Z`, capture the `Personnel roster` table header buttons (`Sort by Rank`, `Sort by Name`, ...) and the initial Name order. Click `Sort by Name` and require the reversed order and changed sort indicator, click it again to restore ascending, then sort by MOS (every fixture Person shares one rank, so Rank cannot show a reorder). Filter with the searchbox (`Baker`) and require only Baker's row, then clear with focused `Control+A`/`Backspace`. Use Baker's row `Details` action and require Baker's record route; return through `Personnel`.

8. **Group header pin and collapse.** As `manager` in `Group` view, read each header's two buttons: `Pin Alpha` (`aria-pressed="false"`, `data-state="off"`, title `Pin group to top`) and `Collapse Alpha` (`aria-expanded="true"`). The pin is the canonical `Toggle` (`data-slot="toggle"`, default size, 36x32 measured: `h-8 min-w-8 px-2.5`), not a 28x28 raw button. Preserve `$V rows user_pinned_groups orgA user_id MANAGER_ID`, then arm `$V network arm-observe PAGE pinned-groups` and click `Pin Bravo`: `aria-pressed` becomes `true`, the label `Unpin Bravo` and title `Unpin group`, Bravo's header moves above Alpha's, and `network state` records one request with status 200 and the saved row names Bravo. Reload: Bravo stays first and still pressed. Click `Unpin Bravo`: original order and `aria-pressed="false"` return, and the saved rows match the preserved readback. Click `Collapse Alpha`: `aria-expanded="false"`, the label `Expand Alpha`, Alpha's rows leave the table and its count badge remains. Click `Expand Alpha` and require the rows back. Reload with Alpha collapsed and record that every group is expanded again (collapse is page state, not saved). Keyboard: Tab to `Pin Alpha` then `Collapse Alpha` with a visible focus ring, operate each with Enter and with Space, and require focus to stay on the same toggle after it flips. Enter, Space and a mouse click on a pin or an Unpin all keep focus on that group's pin (`data-pin-group`) after the group moves, checked once while a held `arm-hold` save keeps the reorder on screen and again after release; a sampler that logs `document.activeElement` never reads `body`. Switch to `A-Z` and back: pin order is retained. Pin failure recovery stays in the [Manage groups recipe](manage-groups.md#personal-group-pins). Do the same at 390 dark and require the headers readable and tappable, with no page-level horizontal scroll.
9. **Roster states and URL.** Empty: as `personnelEmpty` open Personnel and capture `No personnel on the active roster` or the access result the actor actually gets, with Add Person absent. Add Person appears in that state only for an actor who can add; record per actor. No match: type a name nobody has and require `No personnel match your search.`; clear with focused `Control+A`/`Backspace`. Restricted: sign in as `ictViewer` (Personnel none) and require `Access Restricted` with the permission message and no roster. Disabled: in `Archive` mode with a selected row, `Confirm Archive`, `Select shown` and `Clear` disable while an archive is held through `arm-hold PAGE personnel/archive-batch`. Loading: Personnel loads in the Organization layout before the page renders, and no control holds that load, so record UNAVAILABLE with the attempted route unless a skeleton is observed on navigation. URL: record that search text, view mode, sort, pins and collapse leave the URL unchanged, and that Details opens `/org/<org>/personnel/<slug>`. Capture 1280 light and 390 dark, including the `Personnel controls` toolbar at 390: one row with the search field, `More Personnel controls` and `Add Person`, and no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth` equals the viewport). `Import`, `Archive`, `Manage Groups` and the `A-Z`/`Group` view fold into More in that order (at about 1008, 956, 860 and 450px), so at 390 choose them from More. In `Archive` mode the `Bulk archive controls` toolbar keeps `Select shown`, `Cancel` and `Confirm Archive` on the row at 390 and moves `Clear` into `More Bulk archive controls` (it folds at about 404px).

Click it: the browser lands on the 403 page (`You don't have permission to view this page.`) after one `<slug>/__data.json` request with `x-sveltekit-invalidated=00011`. In that response the `[slug]` layout node holds the Person and the `[slug]` page node is `{"type":"error","status":403}` (`This requires Personnel can manage permissions`). `personnelOperate` reproduces it in the unscoped case. `perfScopedLeader` and `personnelScoped` (configure) open the record.

## Gotchas

- `Edit` and `Details` repeat on each row. Choose the UID within the matching person's row.
- The fixture's `viewer` and `scoped` actors can read the roster but may not open a Personnel Record, and the roster still shows `Details` to every operate-level actor (step 10). Use `manager` for the record-opening step.
- `manager` deliberately starts with no Calendar grant for authorization checks. Temporarily grant it Calendar `view` for the integrated readiness check, then restore `none`. A missing availability summary under the default grant does not test the Calendar-backed read.
- Search results can change the visible rows without changing the URL; capture the search value and rows in the same snapshot.
- `Import`, `Add Person`, and archive are separate mutation journeys; their presence in the toolbar does not prove them.
- For roster load and search timing at 20/100/400 People, use the [performance recipe](performance.md); above 250 People the By Group view pauses rendering.
- A group header's two controls repeat per group; name them by their `aria-label` (`Pin Alpha`, `Collapse Alpha`) rather than by position. The pin is the canonical `Toggle` and the collapse is a `Button`; record their focus ring and accessible names exactly as rendered.
