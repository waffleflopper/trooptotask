The Questions That Matter

Answer
faster.
Brief
better.

Every formation, every brief, every handoff — the same questions come up. TroopToTask puts the answers one click away, not buried in four different spreadsheets.

01
Who is here today?
Real-time accountability view. Sorted by platoon, section, or unit. Updated when statuses change — not when someone remembers to update a spreadsheet.
02
Who is unavailable and why?
Leave, TDY, medical, school, profile, appointment, custom — each tracked with expected return date and authorized reason visible to leaders with access.
03
What duties are filled or uncovered?
Daily duty assignment board shows every position — CQ, SDO, details — alongside who is assigned, gaps flagged, and the next scheduled rotation.
04
What training is coming due?
Track certifications, recurring training requirements, and readiness deadlines. See what expires this week, this month, or for a specific individual.
05
Where are the personnel gaps?
Readiness summary surfaces availability rate by element, understrength billets, and personnel pending action — at a glance, not a data pull.
06
What needs action before the next brief?
Expiring evals, pending counselings, open onboarding tasks, and unfiled documents — consolidated into a prioritized action queue for each leader.
Platform Capabilities

Built for
unit work.

Not adapted from a corporate project management tool. Not a generic HR system repurposed for military use. TroopToTask is built around the actual workflows of unit leadership — from roster management to rating scheme tracking.

CAP 01
Personnel Roster
Manage personnel organized by platoon, section, or custom group. Track assignment, position, and contact data. Export to standard formats on demand.
CAP 02
Availability Calendar
Visual unit calendar showing who is present, on leave, TDY, at school, or on profile. Navigate by date range or filter by element to answer accountability questions immediately.
CAP 03
Status Tracking
Structured statuses — present, leave, TDY, school, appointment, profile, and custom — each with start date, expected return, authorizing leader, and remarks.
CAP 04
Duty Assignment
Plan and assign daily duties: CQ, SDO, details, and any custom position. See today's roster against available personnel, flag gaps, and maintain a duty history log.
CAP 05
Training & Readiness
Track certifications, recurring training requirements, and readiness deadlines by individual. Get visibility on what is current, expiring, or overdue across the unit.
CAP 06
Counseling Records
Maintain counseling and leader's book entries by Soldier. Track initial, periodic, and event-driven counselings. Flag overdue requirements. Exportable for continuity.
CAP 07
In-Processing & Onboarding
Structured workflows for in-processing new arrivals. Checklist-driven tasks assigned across sections. Track completion status and hold leaders accountable to deadlines.
CAP 08
Rating Scheme Tracking
Monitor OER, NCOER, and WOER timelines. Track rating chain assignments, scheduled dates, and submission status. Surface upcoming and overdue evaluations by unit.
CAP 09
Permissions & Audit
Role-based access controls align with unit structure. Full audit log tracks who made what change and when. Multi-organization support for higher and adjacent units.
