# Public and marketing pages

A visitor reads the product, About, Security, Privacy and Terms pages without an account, switches the theme, and reaches Sign In and Request Access. A signed-in member sees the same pages with `Dashboard` in place of the access links. The pages are static prose: they make no commercial claim (pricing, plans, subscription, billing, trial, seats, checkout) and no Duty Roster claim.

## Sub-features

- `public-nav`: header with brand link home, `About` and `Security` links (active on their own page), `FAQ` on home only, `Toggle theme`, `Sign In` and `Request Access`.
- `home-overlay-nav`: on `/` the header sits over the hero in the overlay style and switches to the solid style once the hero has scrolled past.
- `narrow-nav`: below 900px the header keeps the brand, `Toggle theme`, `Sign In` and `Request Access`; there is no mobile menu, and the footer carries About, Security, FAQ, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
- `theme`: light/dark toggle that persists across reload and across pages.
- `faq`: six home questions, one answer open at a time, mouse and keyboard.
- `footer-links`: seven links that resolve.
- `contact-cards`: `mailto:` links on Security, Privacy and Terms.
- `signed-in-state`: `Dashboard` in the header and `Go to Dashboard` as the call to action on `/` and the inner pages.
- `public-claims`: no commercial or Duty Roster wording on any public page.
- `public-appearance`: Home, About, Security, Privacy and Terms at 1280x800 and 390x844, light and dark, no horizontal overflow, no console error.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the site root signed out. `About` and `Security` are in the header; `Privacy Policy` and `Terms of Use` are in the footer, as are About, Security, FAQ, Request Access and Sign In. `Request Access` and `Sign In` also appear in the header, in the home hero and in the closing call to action. A signed-in member opens the same URLs and sees `Dashboard` instead.

## Driving it with verify:app

Use the origin from `doctor`. These pages are static, so loading, empty, error and denied states do not apply. The only access state is signed out versus signed in. Public tabs use `tool new_page` in a fresh context; the signed-in tab comes from `login viewer`. Reuse [sign in](sign-in.md) for `/auth/login` and the sign-in form itself, and [identity recovery](identity-recovery-security.md) for the Request Access form.

Public pages scroll inside the public shell (an `overflow:auto` child of a full-height layout), not the window: `window.scrollTo` does nothing, `documentElement.scrollHeight` equals the viewport, and `fullPage` screenshots show only one viewport. Find the shell without relying on class names: `const shell = [...document.querySelectorAll('body *')].find((e) => /auto|scroll/.test(getComputedStyle(e).overflowY) && e.scrollHeight > e.clientHeight + 1)`, then scroll with `shell.scrollTo(0, y)`. "The shell" below means this element. The catch-all rate limit allows 60 requests per minute per address, counting every page load and client navigation; pace page loads at least 3 seconds apart, and if a page body is `{"error":"Too many requests"}` wait 60 seconds and reload before recording anything.

Commands below use `PAGE` and `$ORIGIN`. Helpers: `evaluate_script` for read-only reads, `resize_page` for the viewport, `take_screenshot`, `press_key` for Tab, Enter and Space, `list_console_messages` for errors.

1. **Public header, signed out.** Open `$ORIGIN/` at 1280x800 (`resize_page`). Expect the brand link to `/`, `About`, `Security`, `FAQ`, `Toggle theme`, `Sign In` and `Request Access` in the header, and no `Dashboard`. Click `Sign In` and expect `/auth/login`; go back, click `Request Access` and expect `/auth/request-access`. On `/about` and on `/security` expect the header's `About` or `Security` link respectively to carry the active state (accent-colored bottom border, full-strength text and `aria-current="page"`) and the other not, and no `FAQ` link. On `/privacy` and `/terms` expect neither active. Clicking the brand from any inner page returns to `/`.

2. **Theme.** Click `Toggle theme` once and expect `document.documentElement.dataset.theme` to flip between `light` and `dark` and `localStorage.theme` to match. Reload and expect the same theme without a flash to the other one. Navigate through the header (`About`, then `Security`) and by full navigation to `/privacy`; expect the theme to hold on each. Toggle back and expect the first theme.

3. **Home overlay navigation.** At scroll 0 on `/` expect the page's first `nav` in the overlay style: `position:fixed`, no visible bottom border, translucent dark chrome background over the hero (about 88% opaque), light header text. Scroll the shell past the hero (`shell.scrollTo(0, hero.offsetHeight + 100)`, wait 800 ms) and expect the solid style: an opaque page-background surface, a visible bottom border and a shadow. Scroll back to 0 and expect the overlay style again. Read computed `position`, `backgroundColor`, `borderBottomColor` and `boxShadow` with `evaluate_script`, and take a viewport screenshot of each state.

4. **Narrow header (390x844).** `resize_page` to 390x844 and reload each public page. Expect the header to show the brand, `Toggle theme`, `Sign In` and `Request Access` only: `About`, `Security` and `FAQ` are hidden below 900px and there is no mobile menu. Expect the footer to carry `About`, `Security`, `FAQ`, `Privacy Policy` and `Terms of Use` as reachable links (scroll to the bottom and read them). Expect the shell's `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` and no element (outside the Security page's `On this page` strip, which scrolls sideways inside itself) with `getBoundingClientRect().right > innerWidth`, on every public page. The document-level `scrollWidth` check is vacuous here because the shell clips horizontal overflow. Expect each header control's rect to lie inside `0..innerWidth`. Record the actual header text and footer link list.

5. **FAQ.** On `/` use the header `FAQ` link and expect the FAQ section in view with `#faq` in the URL. Expect six question buttons, all `aria-expanded="false"` with their answers not visible (hidden or not rendered). Click question 1: it becomes `aria-expanded="true"` and its answer is visible. Click question 2: question 2 opens and question 1 closes, so never more than one is open. Click the open question: it closes and none is open. Tab to a question and press Enter, then Space on another: each toggles as with a click. Expect a visible focus ring on the focused trigger: computed `outlineStyle` not `none` with `outlineWidth` above 0, or a non-`none` `boxShadow` ring.

6. **Footer links.** At 1280 scroll to the footer and list its links with `evaluate_script`. Expect exactly seven: `About` `/about`, `Security` `/security`, `FAQ` `/#faq`, `Request Access` `/auth/request-access`, `Sign In` `/auth/login`, `Privacy Policy` `/privacy`, `Terms of Use` `/terms`. A same-origin `fetch` of each path returns 200. Click `Privacy Policy` and `Terms of Use` and expect their headings.

7. **Contact cards.** Read every `a[href^="mailto:"]` on `/security`, `/privacy` and `/terms`. Expect Security to have `mailto:security@trooptotask.org?subject=Security%20vulnerability%20report`, Privacy to have a card for `privacy@trooptotask.org`, and Terms to have a card for `support@trooptotask.org`. Inline `privacy@trooptotask.org` links in Privacy and Terms body text are also `mailto:`. Do not click; record the hrefs.

8. **Keyboard.** On `/` at 1280 press Tab from the top and, after each press, read `document.activeElement` (tag, text or aria-label, href) and its computed outline and box shadow. Expect: brand, `About`, `Security`, `FAQ`, `Toggle theme`, `Sign In`, `Request Access` in visible left-to-right order, then the hero's call to action, each with a visible focus ring. Repeat at 390x844 and expect the order brand, `Toggle theme`, `Sign In`, `Request Access`, then the hero call to action. Repeat on `/about` and `/security` for the header part.

9. **Signed-in state.** `login viewer`, then `navigate_page` the signed-in page to `$ORIGIN/`. Expect the header to show `Dashboard` (to `/dashboard?show=all`) and not `Sign In` or `Request Access`, and the hero and closing call to action to read `Go to Dashboard` and not `Request Access`. Repeat on `/security` (header and closing call to action) and on `/about`; expect no `Request Access` or `Sign In` call to action in the page body, and About's second body action to read `Go to Dashboard`. The footer is the same static seven links in both states. Click `Dashboard` and expect `/dashboard?show=all`, the signed-in Organization chooser, not a login form. Screenshot signed-in home at 1280 light.

10. **Claims scan.** For each of the five pages, run `evaluate_script` with `() => document.body.innerText` and save the text to `<page>-text.txt` in the run directory. Search it case-insensitively for `pricing|price|plans|subscri|billing|trial|free tier|seat|checkout|invoice|per month|duty roster|duty assign|rotation|tier` and `\$\d`. Expect no match that makes a commercial or Duty Roster claim. A match is read in context and dispositioned; "the plan" as everyday English is not a claim, "duty assignments" listed as a product feature is.

11. **Appearance matrix.** For each width (1280x800, 390x844) and theme (light, dark) and each page (home, about, security, privacy, terms): `resize_page`, set the theme with `Toggle theme`, `navigate_page`, reveal the page, then capture it as described below into `<page>-<width>-<theme>.png`. That is 20 captures. **Reveal.** Home hides each `.reveal` section until an IntersectionObserver sees it, so a capture of an unrevealed page is blank in places. Immediately before the capture run `evaluate_script` with an async function that steps the shell's `scrollTo(0, y)` down its `scrollHeight` in increments of `innerHeight / 2`, waits 150 ms per step and 600 ms at the bottom, scrolls back to 0, waits 600 ms and returns `document.querySelectorAll('.reveal:not(.revealed)').length`. Expect 0 before capturing; the same helper runs harmlessly on the other pages. **Full-page capture.** A tall viewport changes the layout (the page spaces sections in `vh` units), so stitch instead: take a viewport screenshot at scroll 0, then repeatedly scroll the shell to `previousBottom - 56` (clamped to the end), wait 700 ms, take a viewport screenshot, drop its top `previousBottom - actualScrollTop` rows (the sticky header and the overlap), and append. The stitched height equals the shell's `scrollHeight`; the sticky `On this page` strip on Security repeats at each seam and a 1 px seam line can appear. Review each capture for clipping, overlapping text and legibility of text over its background in both themes.

12. **Console.** After each page load in the matrix, call `list_console_messages` and save it. Expect no `error` message. The owned app is not hosted on Vercel, so every page also logs one or two errors for `/_vercel/insights/script.js` and `/_vercel/speed-insights/script.js` (HTML MIME type or 404) and matching Vercel `log` lines. Record these as environment noise and list them separately; any other error is a failure.

13. **Contrast.** On each page, in both themes at 1280, run `evaluate_script` over visible text nodes: resolve the text color and the nearest opaque ancestor background (use a 1x1 canvas to convert `oklch()` colors), and list text under 4.5:1 (3:1 for 24px or 18.66px bold), skipping text inside `aria-hidden="true"` decoration (the home quote mark, the classification bar's `//`). Expect none. Exclude the overlay header on `/` over the dark hero, whose translucent background makes the nearest-ancestor method wrong; judge it from the screenshot.

## Gotchas

- Theme lives in `localStorage` under `theme` and is applied to `html[data-theme]`; a fresh browser context starts from the system preference (light in the owned headless browser). Toggle once per theme change, because the toggle is the only control.
- The home page's `.reveal` classes and the header's solid state depend on IntersectionObserver; a screenshot before scrolling can show hidden sections or the overlay header.
- The home header's overlay colors differ from the inner pages' solid header. The hero is dark chrome in both themes; judge contrast of header text over it in the overlay state separately from the solid state.
- Public pages never fetch Organization data; a signed-in tab only changes the header and call-to-action state. A session that has not finished MFA is treated as signed out.
