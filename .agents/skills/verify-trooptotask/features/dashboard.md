# Dashboard composition and freshness

The Organization Dashboard composes authorized Personnel, Calendar, Training, Critical Tasks, Onboarding and Rating facts. A source's permission remains independent; active scoped Personnel supply strength, while the Rating due projection omits archived People's evaluations (the stored rows remain). The shell must remain usable while the workspace/summary load, and old responses must never restore another Organization's facts.

## Sub-features

- `strength-band`: Bottom line, the 14-day forecast with its floor line and flagged duty days, and the by-group strip, all from one per-day status index over the loaded window. Steps are under [Strength band](#strength-band).
- `quick-add`: Status, Training record and Start onboarding beside the title open the Calendar, Training and Onboarding dialogs, and each save shows on the Dashboard without a reload. Steps are under [Quick add](#quick-add).
- `needs-you-actions`: Update status, Record class, Fill and Award started act on a Needs you row in place, and a saved row leaves the queue. Check off opens Onboarding at the packet. Steps are under [Actions](#actions).
- `combined-overview`: current strength, availability, Person/Group assignments, Training attention/currency, Critical Tasks readiness, evaluations, Onboarding and nearest ETS/DEROS use independently known source facts. Steps for the Readiness row are under [Readiness row](#readiness-row).
- `source-admission`: full, Alpha-only, empty-Group and no-lane members have different authorized projections; an ordinary foreign member cannot read A.
- `readiness-data`: the Critical Tasks summary is authorized and scoped, and the Critical tasks column renders it. A member without Critical Tasks access gets neither the column nor the read. Inspect the ordinary page response separately from the visible column.
- `reload-and-consumers`: counts persist after reload and navigation to source pages; hydration cannot corrupt the source workspaces.
- `navigation-recovery`: an exact A Dashboard navigation fails once, remains recoverable, and a delayed A navigation cannot overwrite B.
- `feature-states`: a feature that is off contributes nothing and its sources are not read; a feature that is on but not set up is hidden for everyone and gives owners and admins a dismissible hint. Steps are under [Features off or not set up](#features-off-or-not-set-up).
- `deferred-state`: first-paint shell, workspace/summary pending, rejection and scope replacement. Whole-navigation holds do not prove individual server-source delay or failure.

After changing request composition, run the real Organization Home route with Critical Tasks access and inspect its settled summary as well as the visible Dashboard. Tests that mock the summary operation cannot prove the route selected every required provider. The Dashboard must settle without its load-error alert.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in, enter an Organization and choose **Home** in its sidebar. The Organization breadcrumb also returns to this Dashboard. Its source workspaces remain available through Calendar, Personnel, Evals, Training, ICTLs and Onboard when the member has the corresponding access. Use **Switch organization** to select another membership; it preserves the current section, so choose Home afterward when switching from Calendar.

## Gotchas

Starter pack apply has an authenticated endpoint and screen-model method, but no rendered Apply control. When changing its request composition, verify the ordinary signed-in `POST /org/ORG_ID/api/starter-configuration-packs/first-week-operations/apply` separately from native Dashboard interaction. An Organization owner must create "Complete unit orientation" as a Training step linked to "Unit Orientation"; reload and reapply must skip existing artifacts without duplicates. A view-only actor must receive 403 with unchanged Types/Templates/Steps. Preserve provider readbacks and remove only recorded synthetic artifacts through the ordinary delete endpoints before seed cleanup. These observations establish API/provider behavior; they do not establish a native Apply journey.

- Page-data responses include session credentials. Redact access/refresh token values before saving or sharing captures, including flattened credential slots; preserve all source facts and error outcomes.
- SvelteKit can return HTTP 200 with serialized route errors. Foreign denial must inspect the node status/message and absence of A data, not only the transport status.
- The switcher preserves Calendar when used there. Enter B's Home before releasing the held A Dashboard response; whole-navigation isolation does not prove individual deferred-source isolation.
- Active packet progress excludes the retired step, so it is two of three (67%), not 75%.
- For load time, requests, bytes and queries on a 20/100/400-Person Organization, use the [performance recipe](performance.md) instead of timing this fixture.

## Frame

The page opens with a header and three bands, each under a 2px rule. The eyebrow reads `DASHBOARD · <UNIT> · <N> PERSONNEL` once the data is in and drops the count while loading. The title is the viewer day, such as `THURSDAY 22 OCT`. Quick add appears beside the title only when an active section offers an entry. `Strength` holds Bottom line (400px) and the 14-day forecast with the by-group strip under it, `Action` holds Needs you and On duty (340px), and `Readiness` holds one column per active section that has one, in the order Training, Critical tasks, Evaluations, Arriving and leaving. Below 1024px the right-hand column stacks under the left. Needs you shows up to nine rows; from ten it shows seven and a fold row whose `Show all N` expands in place. A row narrower than 672px puts its label and timing on the first line. On duty holds Tonight and tomorrow, one row per Calendar lane in lane order, and Coming and going for the next seven days. A section whose feature is switched off contributes no row, lane, column or button.

While the workspace or summary is pending, each band shows skeletons shaped like its content and one visually hidden `role="status"` reads `Loading Dashboard`. A failed summary source keeps every band: the Training and Critical tasks columns each read `Couldn't load <subject>. Reload the page to try again.` for a viewer with that access, and Needs you says `Needs you is incomplete. Training items could not load.` under its rows, or in place of `Nothing needs you right now.`. It names only failed columns whose section feeds Needs you, so Critical tasks never appears there. One `Retry Dashboard` alert sits above the bands; a viewer who reads neither summary source sees neither the note nor the alert. A failed background refresh after a successful load keeps the last values and shows nothing. A failed workspace source is caught by the Dashboard layout, which replaces the whole page. Steps live in [Dashboard and Training states](training-form-states.md#driving-it-with-verifyapp).

## Viewer day

With a browser zone whose day differs from UTC (see the [skill](../SKILL.md#launch)), reload the Dashboard after signing in. The title and the breadcrumb both name the browser day, and both come from the page load, not the browser clock. Strength, Tonight and tomorrow, and Coming and going must agree with Calendar's `TODAY` line, and the Training column must agree with the Training pages on the browser day; a record expiring on that day is not counted expired. The Critical tasks column must agree with the Critical Tasks pages.

## Strength band

A Person counts once a day. When entries overlap, the status type first in catalog order wins, then the entry that started first. Assigned is the active scoped roster. A duty day is a weekday that is not a stored holiday or closure in `special_days`; the bootstrap reads those in the same `Promise.all` as the other workspace sources, so a `special_days` hold or `fail-hard` behaves like `availability_entries` in step 8 and a failure replaces the page through the Dashboard layout. A duty day below the Organization's strength floor (Settings › Features, 80% by default) is flagged: amber count and label, and one amber band across each stretch of adjacent flagged columns. Weekends and closures stay dimmed and are never flagged, even below the floor, so a run that crosses a weekend shows two bands while the headline counts it as one run. A flagged today turns the hero number amber. The range and lowest day in `Steady` and `N to M available` come from duty days only, and a flagged duty day always outranks `Steady`.

- The forecast flags today and every other duty day Baker is out; the headline reads `Below 80% today, back above on` the first duty day after Baker returns, by weekday alone when it is within a week. The legend lists Verification Training and `80% floor`.
- The by-group strip reads `BRAVO 0/1` (amber) then `ALPHA 2/2`. `Open calendar` opens A's Calendar on the month of the Dashboard's today, even if Calendar was left on another month.
- `dashboardScoped` reads `2`, `of 2`, `AVAILABLE · 100%`, `Everyone is available today.` and `Steady: 2 available every day`, and the strip shows only `ALPHA 2/2`.

Scale comes from the workloads. On 2026-10-21 `perfWide` read `15 of 20` (`small`, one block per Person, names and return dates, `ALPHA 7/10` amber and `BRAVO 8/10`), `86 of 100` (`typical`, one proportional bar, one row per status with a count, the two largest source Groups and `N more` Groups, an `All 14 names` toggle, and `By group · all 5 · lowest first`) and `295 of 400` (`large`, `By group · lowest 5 of 16` and `All 16 groups`). Workload dates move with the run, so record the observed figures rather than expecting these.

## Readiness row

Each column has a title, a big figure, a bar and a note, then a link to its owning page. Bar shares are green for good, amber for warning and red only for the worst. Columns share the width equally and close ranks when a section contributes none. From 640 to 1023 they wrap two to a row; below 640 they stack.

- **Training** is Person-based currency: a Person with any expired or orange record is not current. The figure is green at 90% or more, amber from 75% and red below. The bar splits current, expiring (orange only) and expired People. The note names the lowest Group when two or more Groups have People and that Group is below 100%.
- **Critical tasks** shows the summary's overall readiness as `N%` ready, with the 30-day change in points. The bar splits the People with an applicable task into three disjoint shares: below the 70% threshold (red), at or above it with a task lapsing soon (amber), and ready (green). The note gives the People below the threshold and the tasks lapsing within the window.
- **Evaluations** shows the overdue count, red above zero. The bar splits overdue, due within 30 days and current, counted on the viewer day, so an evaluation due in 45 days is current. The note gives those due within 30 and within 60 days and the current count, which includes the 31 to 60 day ones.
- **Arriving and leaving** shows the losses in the loss window, a tick per loss on a line the length of the window, and the nearest three by name. With Onboarding access it adds the active packet count and links to Onboarding; otherwise it links to Personnel.

## Quick add

Each active section contributes its entries in order: Status (Calendar availability, `calendar.manageAvailability`, only once a status type exists), Training record (`training.recordCompletions`, only when an actionable Person and Training type pair is not exempt) and Start onboarding (`onboarding.manageWorkflows`). An entry needs a loaded Person the viewer may act on. For Start onboarding that Person must have no active packet, unless the viewer may create Personnel and can add the arrival. If the dialog chunk fails to load, a toast appears and the next click tries again. Start onboarding is the one filled button; without it every entry is outlined, as in frame 05. With no entry, the `Quick add` label is absent too. Status and Training record open a picker (`Add status`, `Add training record`) whose `Person` combobox lists only People the viewer may act on, by last name, then the shared `Set Personnel Status` dialog, or `Add Training Record` (`Edit Training Record` when the Person already has a record of that type). Status starts on the viewer day. A Person exempt from the chosen Training type keeps `Continue` off, with a note that exemptions change on the Training page. Start onboarding opens `Start Onboarding` directly. None of these reads anything at page load: Training record reads the Person's records on `Continue` and Start onboarding reads templates and ranks (`GET api/onboarding/start-options`) as it opens.

## Actions

A Needs you row offers one action when the viewer may take it; otherwise only its link remains. While an action opens or saves, its button reads `aria-disabled="true"` and ignores a second press. A failed save keeps the row and shows the message in it, as a `role="alert"` line or inside the open dialog or panel, with no toast. A saved row leaves the queue and focus moves to the action, or else the link, of the row now in its place, or to the empty state (the incomplete note when the summary failed) once the queue is empty. Closing a dialog or panel returns focus to its button. None of these reads anything at page load.

## Features off or not set up

Settings › Features (`settings/features`) stores the unit's switches in `organizations.disabled_features`. Availability and Calendar cannot be off. A feature that is off adds no Needs you row, readiness column, Quick add entry, On duty block or forecast lane, and its sidebar link is gone. The bootstrap, the page summary and `GET /org/ORG_ID/api/dashboard/summary` skip its reads, and each read also needs the viewer's lane: a member whose Onboarding lane is `none` gets no packets even though Personnel access would let the provider return them.

A feature that is on but holds nothing is not set up: Onboarding with no template, Training with no training type, Critical Tasks with no active task (`ictlSummary.hasActiveTasks` false; a failed summary is unknown, not empty) and Evaluations with no rating scheme entry. Its section adds nothing for anyone, so Start onboarding goes and Arriving and leaving drops its onboarding sentence and links to Personnel. Owners and admins also see one dashed `Admins only` hint per gap under the readiness columns, reading `<Feature> is on, but <gap>, so it isn't shown here.` with a fix link, `Turn off <feature>` to `settings/features`, and `Dismiss the <Feature> hint`. Dismissal is kept in this browser under `t2t:dashboard-setup-hints:<orgId>:<userId>`, and focus moves to the next hint. With no column and no hint left, the Readiness band is omitted.
