# Onboarding and Sign-in Rosters: raw controls and screen states

A member working an Onboarding packet or a Sign-in Roster can reach every control by keyboard, sees which item is selected or expanded, and gets a clear loading, empty, error and denied state on every screen at desktop and phone widths, in light and dark.

## Sub-features

- `board-controls`: the Onboarding board's packet cards, the detail sheet's actions, the paperwork stage queue, the checkbox steps, the `Completed` section toggle, the completed paperwork `Expand to adjust` toggle and an inactive step's `Remove` expose a name, a role, keyboard operation, a visible app focus ring and their state (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `aria-current` or state text, `disabled`).
- `board-roles`: Mark Complete, Cancel, Re-sync, Switch Template, Assign Template and Start Onboarding follow the actor's Onboarding role; view members can open a packet but cannot change it.
- `history-controls`: History's packet accordion, read-only paperwork stage indicators and the notes collapsible.
- `template-controls`: the empty template's `Add first step` action, step `Move up` and `Move down` by keyboard, the type-management list and detail, and view-role denial.
- `roster-controls`: the Sign-in Roster modal's saved-roster row toggle, create and `Generate & Print`, signed-scan upload through the native file input, download, remove and delete confirmation; viewer denial; list failure with retry; empty list.
- `screen-states`: loading, empty, error, denied, disabled, 1280x800 light and 390x844 dark for the board, History, Templates and the roster modal.
- `workspace-load-error`: a rejected Onboarding workspace source shows one `WorkspaceLoadError` (`Unable to load Onboarding` with `Retry Onboarding`), never a stuck skeleton.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose `Onboard` in Organization navigation for the board. Choose a packet card to open its detail sheet. `History` and `Templates` are in the board header. Sign-in Rosters: `Training`, then `Sign-In Rosters` in the page actions (or `More training actions` at 390).

## Driving it with verify:app

Expected results come from #1604's acceptance, the [foundation contract](../../../../docs/ui-foundation-provenance.md#canonical-category-mapping), the [component contracts](component-foundation.md) and the retained behavior in the [packet](onboarding-packets.md), [template](onboarding-templates.md) and [roster](sign-in-rosters.md) recipes. Those recipes stay authoritative for data, permission, scope and failure-recovery steps; this recipe adds the presentation and state checks they do not state. The D06 cutover is presentation-only, so their API, RLS, provider-sized and cross-Organization journeys are not repeated here. Record each cell PASS, FAIL, UNAVAILABLE or UNRUN with the actor, width and theme. A defect stays FAIL until the same step passes on a later candidate.

Preconditions: owned launch and `doctor` pass; use the index's `WORKER`, `V`, fresh snapshots and evidence directory. Save `component-foundation.md`'s state reader as `reader.js`. Dismiss `What's New` (`Got it`) on each actor's first load. Use native `resize_page` (1280x800, 390x844) and the visible `Toggle theme` for dark. Starting a keyboard step with `focus()` on the control and then pressing real `Tab`, `Enter`, `Space` and `Escape` is faster than counting Tab presses. For the focus ring read `getComputedStyle` `outline` and `box-shadow` while the control has `:focus-visible` after a real key press. The app ring is the 3px `ring-ring/50` box-shadow; a UA `auto` outline is the default ring, not the app ring. At each capture read `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` against `innerWidth`.

Packet setup, in this order. As `onboardingOperate`, `Start Onboarding` for Able with the seeded template and read `$V rows personnel_onboardings orgA personnel_id ABLE` (one new in-progress row). Its card sits in `Just Started`. Run `$V seed onboarding-legacy`, then as `onboardingConfigure` open that Person's card and `Assign Template` with `Verification Onboarding A`; the packet then holds three new active steps and inactive `Orientation` (completed), `Paperwork` (Reviewed, incomplete) and `Historic task` (completed). Visibly `Cancel` both packets at the end and run `clean-seeds`; packets have no hard delete.

## Gotchas

- `server-source` controls one GET per arm in the app process; an SPA navigation inside the Onboarding layout does not reload the workspace. Enter `Onboard` from another feature or load the URL fully.
- `network arm-fail` is single use. Run `network restore` after each case.
- The detail sheet opens from component state, not a `Sheet.Trigger`; record exactly where focus lands on close.
- Paperwork stage and checkbox saves are optimistic; read the provider row, not only the moved card.
- UI-created packets cannot be hard-deleted. Cancel them visibly; owned cleanup disposes the provider.
