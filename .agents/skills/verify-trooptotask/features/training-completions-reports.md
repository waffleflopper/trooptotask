# Record Training and check readiness reports

A member records a Soldier's Training, checks the saved completion after reload, and uses the matrix, the Training Reports briefing and the downloaded workbook to find current, expiring and missing requirements within their authorized roster.

## Sub-features

- `completion-lifecycle`: add, edit, cancel and remove a completion through a matrix cell or the Person editor; dates, notes, certificate URL and the existing record ID survive ordinary updates and reload.
- `completion-recovery`: failed saves and removals leave stored facts unchanged, show useful feedback and allow retry; an operate-only deletion request is distinct from a completed deletion.
- `completion-import`: preview valid and invalid CSV rows, correct a row, import selected records, and repeat an import without creating duplicate completion identities.
- `matrix-and-report`: Required and targeted applicability, dated status, the briefing's Bottom line, Forecast, Hot-spot grid, Type risk bars and Focus areas, URL-kept filters and the Affected soldiers drill-down from a cell or focus link agree with independently counted examples; Optional Training remains visible in the matrix without entering required readiness.
- `matrix-export`: the main Training page exports one alphabetical row per authorized Person in the selected Group and every Training type in catalog order, including columns hidden by a saved view. Cells retain saved dates or state exemption, missing, not required, or completion without a date. Expiration-only and completion-only columns identify their meaning in the header.
- `report-export`: the downloaded workbook has the sheets Summary, Forecast, Hot spots, Focus and Requirements for the briefing on screen; Requirements lists every affected requirement of the filtered briefing with status labels and saved dates.
- `report-print`: Print brief captures the loaded briefing and optional loaded detail page together. Print A uses an overview, a transposed matrix with bounded Group or duty-role columns, a compact worst-first type-risk summary, and an optional appendix. Every sheet retains context and white margins. Requirement readiness stays separate from Soldier counts.
- `completion-access`: Training view, operate and configure retain different actions; Alpha-only and foreign-Organization access cannot expose or change other Personnel's completions.
- `reverse-consumers`: Dashboard Training projections, Personnel readiness and the Person workbook retain their distinct meanings; Onboarding consumes completion references through its retained Training contract.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose `Training` in the Organization navigation. Choose a Person's Training cell to add or edit that record, or open the Person editor from the named row. Use `Import` in the Training actions for CSV preview. Use `Export Excel` in those actions for the full training matrix, keeping the selected Group and including every Training column even when a saved view hides some. At narrow widths, open `More training actions` if the action is in overflow. Open `Reports` for the `Training Reports` briefing: the `Training report controls` toolbar (`Filter by view`, `Filter by group`, `Filter by rank`, `Filter by MOS`, `Filter by duty role`, `Filter by training type`, the `Forecast horizon` radios; it wraps onto further rows when narrow), the page-header `Training report actions` (secondary `Export Excel`, primary `Print brief`, plus `Clear filters` when the selection is not the default and an `Updating briefing` status while a change loads), the Bottom line, the Forecast chart (`data-testid="training-briefing-forecast"`, with a screen-reader table of weekly points), the Hot-spot grid (`Rows by` radios; each cell is a button named `<row>, <type>: <n>% ready today[, drops <k> points by <date>][, selected]` holding `[data-slot=readiness-band-cell][data-tier]`), the `Already gone vs. about to go` Type risk table (`Not ready and lapsing requirements by training type`: a row header per type, stacked bar, `Not ready` and `By <date>` counts), and the `Where training time buys the most` Focus list (at most three numbered areas, each with its title heading, sentence, what-if chip and a `View <n> soldier(s)` link; the wave area also states `First lapse <date>`). Choosing a cell or focus link opens the `Affected soldiers` region below, moves focus to it and fills the `Affected soldiers` table (Rank, Name, Group, Training type, Status today, Expiration date, Lapses in horizon) with a status line, a `Rows <a>–<b> of <n>` range, `Rows per page` radios (25, 100) and the table's `Previous`/`Next` buttons when there is more than one page; the open focus link carries `aria-current`. Filters, horizon and `rowsBy` live in the URL, so a reload restores the same briefing. For the reverse Personnel view, choose `Personnel`, the matching Person's `Details`, then inspect `Readiness summary` or choose `Export records`. Return to the Organization Dashboard to inspect its Training summary.

## Gotchas

- Required Training has three applicable People; Targeted has only Able. Optional records can become Current in the matrix while staying outside the report's required-readiness calculation. Do not count N/A as missing. A warning record is labeled Expiring, not Current, yet counts as ready in the report's readiness.
- Date examples are relative to the fixture's local day. Record the exact dates and rerun `fixture` after an owned restart; avoid comparing a later-day status to an earlier baseline expectation.
- The matrix's Focus View and Group selector control presentation. Clear stale selections before counting all three People or all Training columns; configuration and completion permissions remain distinct.
- The existing bulk-import toolbar requires configure access. Operate-only members can record individual completions without seeing Import; do not redefine that affordance during baseline preparation. Group-scoped configuration can retain Import while catalog edits remain denied.
- Save/upsert is keyed by Organization, Person and type; preserve the recorded ID during edits and repeat imports. Notes/certificate readback is needed to distinguish a saved update from an unchanged optimistic row.
- A configure deletion and an operate-only approval request have different outcomes. Capture request status and saved rows before interpreting a disappearing modal; failure handling may itself be a baseline defect.
- Select portal options can be absent from accessibility snapshots. Inspect the visible listbox read-only and use focused keyboard selection, then capture the resulting selected label or row set. Do not call application handlers.
- Activating the selected Horizon, Rows by or Rows per page option again, by pointer or Space, must leave it checked and avoid another request. Choosing a different option still changes the briefing or detail and its URL where applicable.
- Endpoint probes need valid fixture IDs and bodies. PUT uses the saved record ID; DELETE uses `{id}`; batch uses `{records:[{personnelId,trainingTypeId,completionDate,status:'completed'}]}`. A validation error does not prove scope denial.
- Never turn a failed observation into a passing baseline by dropping it. Expiration-only, exemption, warning-yellow boundaries, empty-Group isolation, multiple-page UI, multiple tabs and delayed Organization/session switches need explicit fixtures and additional proof when affected; unrun paths remain named limitations.
- Focused modal/store/import/report/route tests and `supabase/tests/training_record_lane_scope.sql` complement this map. UI/API denial does not substitute for isolated provider/RLS checks, and arithmetic tests do not replace the downloaded workbook.
