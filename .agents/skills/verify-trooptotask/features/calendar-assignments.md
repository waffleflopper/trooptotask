# Manual assignments

A Calendar operator plans Person and Group coverage for a day or month. Saved assignments keep their Organization, date, type and assignee across Calendar, Assignment Planner and report reads; a clear removes only the selected slot.

## Sub-features

- `saved-slots`: the September 15 Person, Group and archived assignments retain their fixture IDs and assignees; Organization B's slot stays private.
- `manual-slot`: set an empty Person and Group slot, reload, change or clear each, and preserve unrelated saved slots.
- `failed-clear`: a refused or failed day-dialog clear restores the saved slot and explains the failure; an explicit retry can clear it.
- `monthly-planning`: navigate months, apply a small Quick Fill batch and clear that type with confirmation.
- `planner-range-retry`: a failed uncovered month load settles with Retry; a successful retry restores editable local assignments.
- `assignment-authorization`: view-only, no-Calendar, Group-scoped and foreign-Organization actors cannot write outside their authority.
- `calendar-lanes`: each shown assignment type is a lane under the Calendar dates, followed by the Days lane; an open day's `+` opens the duty picker, an assigned day and a row marker open `Daily Assignments`, and readers see names without actions.
- `assignment-reentry`: an assignment saved while the Calendar re-entry snapshot is in flight stays visible after it applies; [Calendar navigation](calendar-navigation.md) step 16 drives it.
- `planner-states`: the monthly table, loading, failed save, keyboard and focus, denied and scoped states, and large-roster render at 1280 light and 390 dark.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in with Calendar operation access and choose `Calendar` → `Duty planner`, which opens `Assignment Planner`. The monthly table has one named Person or Group control per active assignment type and date. Click a Calendar date header, an assigned lane day or a row marker to see the `Daily Assignments` modal for that date. An open lane day's `+` opens the duty picker for that type and date. `Reports` shows assignment coverage and other Calendar projections. A Calendar reader may see saved assignments but cannot manage them.

## Gotchas

- Malformed assignment and availability dates and ids are driven in [Invalid input](invalid-input.md); a rejected batch writes no record.
- The Person combobox's suggestions may be visible in the DOM but absent from the accessibility snapshot. Fill its named searchbox, confirm the expected suggestion text through read-only DOM inspection, then use native `ArrowDown` and `Enter`. The Group combobox likewise uses keyboard selection; verify the saved assignee ID because an extra ArrowDown may choose the second Group.
- The occupied Staff slot failed keyboard reassignment on the earlier baseline (`ee8f00a2` and `6e184594`). Recheck the repair by saving Able first, then filtering the occupied slot to Baker and pressing Enter. Require one Baker row after reload, while the unrelated Group and seeded rows remain intact.
- Reloading the Planner opens the current month. Navigate back with `Previous month` before rereading an earlier month's slots.
- The Quick Fill `Select assignment type` list keeps its options out of the accessibility snapshot, and ArrowUp/ArrowDown did not move its keyboard selection reliably. Read `[role=option]` through read-only DOM inspection, then read the trigger text after choosing to confirm which type is active before `Apply`.
- Active Planner controls do not show the archived type. Do not delete or rewrite the seeded September 15 rows during routine slot checks.
- Migration `20260928000100` provides transactional slot replacement. Verify that the application calls it for single, clear and batch writes; ordinary UI saves alone do not prove rollback or serialization. The SQL failure and concurrent-writer suites establish those database properties in an isolated provider.
- Empty-Group availability writes must return `403` with unchanged saved rows. Bulk Add must retain only failed People and the complete draft for an explicit retry; [Bulk status](bulk-status.md) owns that journey. The earlier `500` and lost-selection observations remain historical failures in the D28 evidence. After revoking a Calendar grant, require a settled `Access Restricted` page on entry from outside Calendar and on reload (a switch between Calendar pages keeps the loaded workspace), then restore the grant and require Calendar to load again.
