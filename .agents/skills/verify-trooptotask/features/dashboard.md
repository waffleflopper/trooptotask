# Dashboard composition and freshness

The Organization Dashboard composes authorized Personnel, Calendar, Training, Critical Tasks, Onboarding and Rating facts. A source's permission remains independent; active scoped Personnel supply strength, while the Rating due projection omits archived People's evaluations (the stored rows remain). The shell must remain usable while the workspace/summary load, and old responses must never restore another Organization's facts.

## Sub-features

- `combined-overview`: current strength, availability, Person/Group assignments, Training attention/currency, evaluations, Onboarding and nearest ETS/DEROS use independently known source facts.
- `source-admission`: full, Alpha-only, empty-Group and no-lane members have different authorized projections; an ordinary foreign member cannot read A.
- `readiness-data`: Critical Tasks summary is authorized and scoped even though the compact Overview currently renders no Critical Tasks card. Inspect its ordinary page response separately from visible UI.
- `reload-and-consumers`: counts persist after reload and navigation to source pages; hydration cannot corrupt the source workspaces.
- `navigation-recovery`: an exact A Dashboard navigation fails once, remains recoverable, and a delayed A navigation cannot overwrite B.
- `deferred-state`: first-paint shell, workspace/summary pending, rejection and scope replacement remain explicit obligations. Whole-navigation holds do not prove individual server-source delay or failure.

After changing request composition, run the real Organization Home route with Critical Tasks access and inspect its settled summary as well as the visible Overview. Tests that supply a broad fixture context or mock the summary operation cannot establish that the route selected every required provider. The Dashboard must settle without its load-error alert before this path earns verification credit.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in, enter an Organization and choose **Home** in its sidebar. The Organization breadcrumb also returns to this Dashboard. Its source workspaces remain available through Calendar, Personnel, Training, ICTLs and Onboard when the member has the corresponding access. Use **Switch organization** to select another membership; it preserves the current section, so choose Home afterward when switching from Calendar.

When changing its request composition, verify the ordinary signed-in `POST /org/ORG_ID/api/starter-configuration-packs/first-week-operations/apply` separately from native Dashboard interaction. An Organization owner must create “Complete unit orientation” as a Training step linked to “Unit Orientation”; reload and reapply must skip existing artifacts without duplicates. A view-only actor must receive 403 with unchanged Types/Templates/Steps. Preserve provider readbacks and remove only recorded synthetic artifacts through the ordinary delete endpoints before seed cleanup. These observations establish API/provider behavior; they do not establish a native Apply journey.

- Page-data responses include session credentials. Redact access/refresh token values before saving or sharing captures, including flattened credential slots; preserve all source facts and error outcomes.
- SvelteKit can return HTTP 200 with serialized route errors. Foreign denial must inspect the node status/message and absence of A data, not only the transport status.
- The switcher preserves Calendar when used there. Enter B's Home before releasing the held A Dashboard response; whole-navigation isolation does not prove individual deferred-source isolation.
- The reproduced former Dashboard projection included the retired step (75%); the retained active-step expectation is 67%. Preserve the failing baseline separately from its repaired final observation.
- For load time, requests, bytes and queries on a 20/100/400-Person Organization, use the [performance recipe](performance.md) instead of timing this fixture.

## Frame

The page opens with a header and three bands, each under a 2px rule. The eyebrow reads `DASHBOARD · <UNIT> · <N> PERSONNEL` once the data is in and drops the count while loading. The title is the viewer day, such as `THURSDAY 22 OCT`. Quick add appears beside the title only when an active section offers an entry. `Strength` holds Bottom line (400px) and the 14-day forecast, `Action` holds Needs you and On duty (340px), and `Readiness` holds one column per active section that has one. Below 1024px the right-hand column stacks under the left. Needs you shows up to nine rows; from ten it shows seven and a fold row whose `Show all N` expands in place. A row narrower than 672px puts its label and timing on the first line. On duty holds Tonight and tomorrow, one row per Calendar lane in lane order, and Coming and going for the next seven days. The Strength, Training & Evaluations, Onboarding and Out Today cards still render under the bands until later slices replace them. A section whose feature is switched off contributes no row, lane, column or button.

While the workspace or summary is pending, each band shows skeletons shaped like its content and one visually hidden `role="status"` reads `Loading Dashboard`. A failed summary source keeps the header and replaces the bands with `WorkspaceLoadError`. A failed workspace source is caught by the Dashboard layout, which replaces the whole page. Steps live in [Dashboard and Training states](training-form-states.md#driving-it-with-verifyapp).

## Viewer day

With a browser zone whose day differs from UTC (see the [skill](../SKILL.md#launch)), reload the Dashboard after signing in. The title and the breadcrumb both name the browser day, and both come from the page load, not the browser clock. Strength, Tonight and tomorrow, and Coming and going must agree with Calendar's `TODAY` line, and the Training card must agree with the Training pages on the browser day; a record expiring on that day is not counted expired. The Overview renders no Critical Tasks card, so compare the Critical Tasks summary in the Dashboard page data with the Critical Tasks pages.
