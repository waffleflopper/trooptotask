# Onboarding packets, progress and history

An Organization member starts a Person's Onboarding checklist, records progress and notes, and retains completed or cancelled packets. Onboarding authority is independent of Personnel and Training; each packet inherits its Person's Organization and Group scope.

## Sub-features

- `start`: select an existing Person and configured template, preserve the chosen date, snapshot the steps, and prevent another active packet for that Person. Authorized Personnel creation is a separate operation through Add Person.
- `progress`: toggle checkbox steps, advance or reverse paperwork stages, retain attributed notes, and derive Training completion from the owned Training source.
- `lifecycle-and-history`: cancel, reopen and complete packets, including the explicit warning for incomplete steps; retain packet/progress identities and inspect history after reload.
- `reconciliation`: explicitly resync changed definitions or switch templates, preserving completed work/notes and inactive removed steps; assigning a template to a legacy packet is a separate case.
- `status-and-order`: each active packet's status (Stalled when an open paperwork step has sat at its stage for 7 or more calendar days, Ready when every active step is done, New when none is, otherwise On track), the list order, the waiting strip, search and Group filters, and the header's Soldier count, stalled count and typical days to finish.
- `workspace-reuse`: moving between the Onboarding list, History and Templates keeps the loaded workspace; History and Templates load their own page data on every visit.
- `scope-and-recovery`: view cannot mutate; operate manages scoped packets; configure additionally reconciles templates. Empty scope and foreign-to-target actors cannot read or change packets. Failed actions require visible feedback, retained drafts/state and usable retry; a late A response must not enter B.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose Onboard in Organization navigation. The list orders Soldiers in processing Stalled, Ready, On track, then New. Use `Start onboarding` in the toolbar to select a member, start date and template. Choose a row to open its packet beside the list, or in a sheet below 1280, to record checklist and stage progress, add notes or close it out. `More packet actions` re-syncs, switches or assigns its template, removes an inactive step or cancels the packet. Choose History to inspect retained packets. Reopening is currently supported by the authenticated packet API, without a visible History control. Templates opens the separate configuration workflow.
