# Dashboard composition and freshness

The Organization Dashboard composes authorized Personnel, Calendar, Training, Critical Tasks, Onboarding and Rating facts. A source's permission remains independent; active scoped Personnel supply strength, while the Rating due projection omits archived People's evaluations (the stored rows remain). The shell must remain usable while the workspace/summary load, and old responses must never restore another Organization's facts.

## Sub-features

- `strength-band`: Bottom line, the 14-day forecast with its floor line and flagged duty days, and the by-group strip, all from one per-day status index over the loaded window. Steps are under [Strength band](#strength-band).
- `combined-overview`: current strength, availability, Person/Group assignments, Training attention/currency, Critical Tasks readiness, evaluations, Onboarding and nearest ETS/DEROS use independently known source facts. Steps for the Readiness row are under [Readiness row](#readiness-row).
- `source-admission`: full, Alpha-only, empty-Group and no-lane members have different authorized projections; an ordinary foreign member cannot read A.
- `readiness-data`: the Critical Tasks summary is authorized and scoped, and the Critical tasks column renders it. A member without Critical Tasks access gets neither the column nor the read. Inspect the ordinary page response separately from the visible column.
- `reload-and-consumers`: counts persist after reload and navigation to source pages; hydration cannot corrupt the source workspaces.
- `navigation-recovery`: an exact A Dashboard navigation fails once, remains recoverable, and a delayed A navigation cannot overwrite B.
- `deferred-state`: first-paint shell, workspace/summary pending, rejection and scope replacement remain explicit obligations. Whole-navigation holds do not prove individual server-source delay or failure.

After changing request composition, run the real Organization Home route with Critical Tasks access and inspect its settled summary as well as the visible Dashboard. Tests that supply a broad fixture context or mock the summary operation cannot establish that the route selected every required provider. The Dashboard must settle without its load-error alert before this path earns verification credit.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in, enter an Organization and choose **Home** in its sidebar. The Organization breadcrumb also returns to this Dashboard. Its source workspaces remain available through Calendar, Personnel, Training, ICTLs and Onboard when the member has the corresponding access. Use **Switch organization** to select another membership; it preserves the current section, so choose Home afterward when switching from Calendar.

## Frame

The page opens with a header and three bands, each under a 2px rule. The eyebrow reads `DASHBOARD · <UNIT> · <N> PERSONNEL` once the data is in and drops the count while loading. The title is the viewer day, such as `THURSDAY 22 OCT`. Quick add appears beside the title only when an active section offers an entry. `Strength` holds Bottom line (400px) and the 14-day forecast with the by-group strip under it, `Action` holds Needs you and On duty (340px), and `Readiness` holds one column per active section that has one, in the order Training, Critical tasks, Evaluations, Arriving and leaving. Below 1024px the right-hand column stacks under the left. Needs you shows up to nine rows; from ten it shows seven and a fold row whose `Show all N` expands in place. A row narrower than 672px puts its label and timing on the first line. On duty holds Tonight and tomorrow, one row per Calendar lane in lane order, and Coming and going for the next seven days. A section whose feature is switched off contributes no row, lane, column or button.

While the workspace or summary is pending, each band shows skeletons shaped like its content and one visually hidden `role="status"` reads `Loading Dashboard`. A failed summary source keeps every band: the Training and Critical tasks columns each read `Couldn't load <subject>. Reload the page to try again.` for a viewer with that access, and Needs you has no Expired rows. A failed workspace source is caught by the Dashboard layout, which replaces the whole page. Steps live in [Dashboard and Training states](training-form-states.md#driving-it-with-verifyapp).

## Viewer day

With a browser zone whose day differs from UTC (see the [skill](../SKILL.md#launch)), reload the Dashboard after signing in. The title and the breadcrumb both name the browser day, and both come from the page load, not the browser clock. Strength, Tonight and tomorrow, and Coming and going must agree with Calendar's `TODAY` line, and the Training column must agree with the Training pages on the browser day; a record expiring on that day is not counted expired. The Critical tasks column must agree with the Critical Tasks pages.

## Strength band

A Person counts once a day. When entries overlap, the status type first in catalog order wins, then the entry that started first. Assigned is the active scoped roster. A duty day is a weekday that is not a stored holiday or closure in `special_days`; the bootstrap reads those in the same `Promise.all` as the other workspace sources, so a `special_days` hold or `fail-hard` behaves like `availability_entries` in step 8 and a failure replaces the page through the Dashboard layout. A duty day below the Organization's strength floor (Settings › Features, 80% by default) is flagged: amber count and label, and one amber band across each stretch of adjacent flagged columns. Weekends and closures stay dimmed and are never flagged, even below the floor, so a run that crosses a weekend shows two bands while the headline counts it as one run. A flagged today turns the hero number amber. The range and lowest day in `Steady` and `N to M available` come from duty days only, and a flagged duty day always outranks `Steady`.

- The forecast flags today and every other duty day Baker is out; the headline reads `Below 80% today, back above on` the first duty day after Baker returns, by weekday alone when it is within a week. The legend lists Verification Training and `80% floor`.
- The by-group strip reads `BRAVO 0/1` (amber) then `ALPHA 2/2`. `Open calendar` opens A's Calendar on the month of the Dashboard's today, even if Calendar was left on another month.
- `dashboardScoped` reads `2`, `of 2`, `AVAILABLE · 100%`, `Everyone is available today.` and `Steady: 2 available every day`, and the strip shows only `ALPHA 2/2`.

Scale comes from the workloads. On 2026-10-21 `perfWide` read `15 of 20` (`small`, one block per Person, names and return dates, `ALPHA 7/10` amber and `BRAVO 8/10`), `86 of 100` (`typical`, one proportional bar, one row per status with a count, the two largest source Groups and `N more` Groups, an `All 14 names` toggle, and `By group · all 5 · lowest first`) and `295 of 400` (`large`, `By group · lowest 5 of 16` and `All 16 groups`). Up to 40 People the strip draws a block per Person; above 40 it draws counts. Up to six Groups all show; above six the lowest five show, at any roster size. ## Readiness row

Each column has a title, a big figure, a bar and a note, then a link to its owning page. Bar shares are green for good, amber for warning and red only for the worst. Columns share the width equally and close ranks when a section contributes none. From 640 to 1023 they wrap two to a row; below 640 they stack.

- **Training** is Person-based currency: a Person with any expired or orange record is not current. The figure is green at 90% or more, amber from 75% and red below. The bar splits current, expiring (orange only) and expired People. The note names the lowest Group when two or more Groups have People.
- **Critical tasks** shows the summary's overall readiness as `N%` ready, with the 30-day change in points. The bar splits the People with an applicable task into three disjoint shares: below the 70% threshold (red), at or above it with a task lapsing soon (amber), and ready (green). The note gives the People below the threshold and the tasks lapsing within the window.
- **Evaluations** shows the overdue count, red above zero. The bar splits overdue, due within 60 days and current, counted on the viewer day. The note gives those due within 30 and within 60 days and the current count.
- **Arriving and leaving** shows the losses in the loss window, a tick per loss on a line the length of the window, and the nearest three by name. With Onboarding access it adds the active packet count and links to Onboarding; otherwise it links to Personnel.
